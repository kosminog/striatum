# Worked project: "Dotpay" (invented brand, for illustration only)

## Plan (from the brief)

- **Name / casing:** Dotpay
- **Category:** payroll for small businesses
- **Audience:** owner-operators, 5 to 50 staff, not finance people
- **Voice:** direct but not blunt, warm but not chummy, precise but not cold
- **Single idea:** "paid on the dot"
- **Visual device:** a filled circle that reads as a full stop or a point on a line
- **Usage set:** favicon, iOS/Android icon, web header, invoice PDF (mono), embroidered polo
- **Competitive set:** six payroll and banking marks; four use shields or ticks, two use coins
- **Avoid:** tick, coin, shield, arrow, clock face, gradient, blue-on-white default

## Round 1 — idea

Three devices tried: (a) circle on a baseline, (b) circle as a period after a short
bar suggesting text, (c) circle inside a rounded square. Twelve images each, mono.

| Candidate | Score | Lost on |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| a-07 | 15 | 4 (wobble), 9 (one lockup only) |
| b-03 | 14 | 3 (bar vanishes at 16 px), 4 |
| c-11 | 11 | 5 (looks like a generic app icon), 7 |

**Read-out:** The circle on a baseline reads as "on the dot" without explanation and
survives at 16 px as a distinctive two-element shape. (b) depends on the bar, which
dies small. (c) is a container, not an idea. Continue with (a).

## Round 2 — form family (hold idea)

Varied: solid geometric / monoline / humanist. Seed held from a-07.

Winner: solid geometric, 16/20. Monoline lost 2 points at 32 px where the line and
circle merged. Humanist added charm and lost systemisability.

**Next:** vary composition. Diff: add "circle diameter about one third of the
baseline length, circle touching the baseline from above".

## Round 3 — composition (hold idea, form)

Varied: circle touching baseline / circle overlapping baseline / circle floating
above a gap.

Winner: overlapping, 17/20. The overlap creates a single silhouette, which reads
better small and gives the mark one shape to remember. Lost on row 10 (no wordmark
yet) and row 4 (model's circle is not round).

**Next:** weight.

## Round 4 — weight (hold all)

Varied baseline thickness relative to circle: 1/6, 1/4, 1/3 of diameter.

Winner: 1/4, 18/20. 1/6 vanished at 16 px; 1/3 read as a lozenge.

Stopping rule met (≥17, no zeros). Reverse image search: no matches. Mirrored and
rotated: clean. Silhouette: reads as an exclamation mark upside down, judged
acceptable and noted.

## Wordmark and vector

Set in a sturdy grotesque, lowercase per profile, the circle replacing the dot on
the "i"-less name by sitting after the final letter as a full stop. Redrawn with
one circle and one rounded rectangle: 10 anchor points. System built: primary,
stacked, symbol, wordmark, mono, reversed, app icon, favicon.

## Critic run

One finding: the stacked lockup's clear space was defined in pixels, not as a
multiple of the dot. Fixed. Verdict: ship.
