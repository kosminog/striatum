# Launch announcement (invented product, for illustration only)

**Reader:** a team lead who already uses the product for weekly reporting.
**Stage:** existing customer, reading the changelog email on a phone.
**Promise:** the Monday report builds itself overnight.
**Next step:** turn on the schedule in Settings.

## Before

> ## Introducing Scheduled Exports!
>
> We're thrilled to announce the launch of Scheduled Exports, a powerful new
> feature that takes our industry-leading reporting platform to the next level.
> After months of hard work by our amazing team, you can now leverage
> cutting-edge automation to seamlessly deliver insights whenever you need them.
> We can't wait to see what you build. Learn more.

## After

> ## Scheduled exports: your Monday report, built overnight
>
> Pick a report, a day and a time, and it lands in your inbox or Slack channel
> as a PDF before you sit down. Nothing to click on Monday morning.
>
> ![The schedule dialog with "Weekly pipeline review, Mondays 07:00" filled in]
>
> Schedules run on your workspace's timezone and skip weeks where nothing
> changed, so you get a note instead of an empty report.
>
> **Turn it on:** Settings → Exports → New schedule. Available on every plan
> from today.

## What changed

- The headline names what is new and the outcome, not the company's feelings
  (one promise per piece).
- "Powerful", "industry-leading", "cutting-edge" became a screenshot with a real
  value and one concrete behaviour (specific beats superlative).
- The reader is addressed first, and the piece ends with the exact path to the
  feature (reader first; one clear next step).
