# Logo critique checklist

Score each direction 0, 1 or 2 per row. Show the table when presenting a cull.
Directions scoring under 12 are dropped without discussion.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Single idea | Several competing ideas or none | One idea, muddled by detail | One idea, instantly read |
| 2 | Mono legibility | Falls apart without colour | Readable but weaker | Fully works in one colour |
| 3 | 16 px legibility | Mush | Recognisable shape, detail lost | Clear and distinctive |
| 4 | Large-scale quality | Visible wobble, uneven weight | Minor optical issues | Clean on a grid |
| 5 | Distinct from competitive set | Could be swapped with a competitor | Similar family, different detail | Clearly its own |
| 6 | Accident audit | Unfortunate reading found | Ambiguous | Clean mirrored, rotated, silhouetted |
| 7 | Timelessness | Built on a current trend | Some trend surface | Would have worked ten years ago |
| 8 | Fit with voice adjectives | Contradicts profile | Neutral | Embodies them |
| 9 | Systemisability | Only works as one lockup | Variants possible with effort | Variants fall out naturally |
| 10 | Wordmark craft | Default font, default spacing | Chosen face, unadjusted | Chosen or modified face, kerned by eye |

## How to write a critique

For each surviving direction, one paragraph:

1. What the single idea is, in the audience's words.
2. Where it is strongest (cite rows).
3. The one thing that must change before it could ship.

Avoid "I like" and "feels". Point at the row.
